Company Overview

Marx-Phase Investment Solutions cc is a fully licensed and insured firm based in Okahandja. We utilize a system-based project management approach to ensure consistent delivery. Our team is drawn from various built environment disciplines, allowing us to provide comprehensive solutions from planning to post-construction.

To serve many sectors on the African continent; build a record for global expertise and local knowledge; and add value to communities with development, business project, and construction management services.

To render a quality service on time and cost-effectively. We value our employees as our cornerstone and ensure clients can count on our dependability, drive, and integrity. We aim to become a premier project and construction management company in Africa.
