Environmental Sustainability

Building a Greener Future for Namibia

At MARX-PHASE INVESTMENT SOLUTIONS cc, we recognize our responsibility to the environment. We are committed to implementing sustainable construction practices that minimize our ecological footprint while delivering high-quality infrastructure.

Sustainable Materials

We prioritize the use of eco-friendly building materials, including our innovative eco-beams and sandbag housing concepts.

Waste Management

Strict waste sorting and recycling protocols on all sites to reduce landfill contribution and promote circular economy principles.

Energy Efficiency

Designing and constructing buildings that maximize natural light and ventilation, reducing the need for artificial cooling and heating.

Our Green Initiatives

  • Eco-Housing: Developing low-cost, high-efficiency housing solutions using alternative building technologies.
  • Water Conservation: Implementing water-saving techniques during construction and integrating rainwater harvesting systems in designs.
  • Site Rehabilitation: Ensuring all construction sites are rehabilitated to their natural state or better post-construction.
  • Local Sourcing: Reducing carbon emissions by sourcing materials from local suppliers whenever possible.
