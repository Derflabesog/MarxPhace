Your Questions, Answered

How long does a typical project take?

Depending on size and complexity, projects usually take 6–12 months from planning to handover.

Can I customize my house design?

Absolutely! We work closely with clients to tailor every detail to their preferences.

What is included in the construction cost?

All major materials, labor, project management, and quality inspections are included. Optional add-ons are extra.

Do you provide financing or payment plans?

Yes, flexible payment options are available to suit your needs.

How do you ensure construction quality?

We use high-quality materials, skilled labor, and regular quality checks at each stage.

Can I track the progress of my project?

Yes, clients receive regular updates with photos, reports, and site visits if desired.

What happens if there are changes during construction?

We discuss change requests and update timelines and budgets transparently before proceeding.
